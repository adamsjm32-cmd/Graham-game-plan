GRADE 6 ELA · NOVEL STUDY

Fish in a Tree Practice Quiz

18 multiple-choice questions on Lynda Mullaly Hunt's Fish in a Tree: author's craft, summarizing, character, point of view and theme. Two rounds of 9, about 10 minutes each. Missed questions come back at the end, and every answer shows why.

Test-day plays

  1. Read the question twice. Look for words like NOT, BEST and TWO.
  2. Read ALL the choices before you pick.
  3. Cross out the silly ones first. Usually two are easy to cut.
  4. Pick TWO means two. Count before you move on.
  5. Look back. Quote questions give a page number. If you have the book, flip there and reread the lines around it.
